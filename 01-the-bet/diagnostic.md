**Product: BluRok Vision**

## Scores

### Contextual Moat — 3.5/5
*Workflow depth × switching cost. Would users leave in a weekend if a competitor showed up?*

**Score rationale:**
*BluRok Vision has the foundation for a strong contextual moat because it is not just answering questions. The producer side can become embedded in the operating workflow: cultivation records, environmental data, SOPs, alerts, crop decisions, quality assessment, profitability analysis, staff workflows, inventory, and eventually market intelligence.
The strongest moat comes from accumulated operational context. Once BluRok understands a farm's cultivars, rooms, historical yields, labor, inputs, environmental conditions, disease history, quality outcomes, SOPs, customers, and economics, moving to another system means rebuilding that operating history.*

**Named attacker (from partner challenge):**
*Climate FieldView / Bayer, or a similar incumbent farm-management platform that adds increasingly capable AI and computer vision. In cannabis specifically, a combination of Metrc-integrated platforms + LeafLink/Jane-style market data could attack different portions of the workflow.*

---

### Data Advantage — 3/5
*Proprietary signal that compounds with usage. What do you see that OpenAI doesn't?*

**Score rationale:**
*This is potentially BluRok Vision's strongest long-term advantage, but today it is more of a data thesis than a mature data moat.*

**Named attacker (from partner challenge):**
*OpenAI is the clearest attacker here. If BluRok's intelligence primarily comes from public information plus an LLM, OpenAI can replicate much of the experience. BluRok wins only when the LLM is sitting on top of data OpenAI does not possess.*

---

### Platform Exposure — 4/5
*Encroachment risk × pivot speed. If Apple/Google/OpenAI ships your hero feature native — then what?*

**Score rationale:**
*BluRok currently has substantial platform exposure because several individually impressive features are vulnerable to commoditization.*

**Named attacker (from partner challenge):**
*OpenAI is the primary platform attacker, followed by Google Gemini/Google Lens for visual intelligence. Neither has to build a complete agricultural platform to hurt BluRok; they only need to commoditize enough of its visible "wow" features.*

---

## Top Vulnerability
BluRok's biggest strategic risk is mistaking AI-powered features for the moat before it has captured enough proprietary operational, biological, economic, and consumer outcome data to make the platform difficult to replace.

## Confidence Level
I have high confidence in the problem space and strategic direction, but only medium confidence in the moat today because it still has to be demonstrated through actual producer adoption, longitudinal proprietary data, workflow retention, and measurable decision improvement.
