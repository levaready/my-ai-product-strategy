# Three-Horizon Roadmap & Board Pitch

# Roadmap

## Horizon 1 — Now: Trusted Farm Learning (0–3 months)

*Build the foundation that allows BluRok to learn safely from every recommendation, action, correction, and outcome.*

### Product priorities

- Productionize the current Replit prototype.
- Close the recursive learning loop:
  **Recommendation → User Response → Action → Outcome → Correction**
- Create persistent farm profiles and operational memory.
- Launch confidence tiers, evidence citations, approval controls, and human-review workflows.
- Add model-provider abstraction, deterministic-first routing, evaluation, cost monitoring, and rollback.
- Establish the initial gold evaluation dataset.
- Recruit the first producer design partners.

### Success metrics

- At least **80%** of high-impact recommendations receive an accept, modify, reject, or escalate response.
- At least **60% Verified Outcome Rate** for high-impact recommendations.
- **100%** of material recommendations display confidence and supporting evidence.
- **100%** of triggered high-risk cases enter human review.
- Gold-set accuracy reaches **≥92%** for supported tasks.
- Hallucination rate remains **<1%**.
- Frontier-model traffic remains **≤25%**.
- AI COGS remains **≤10% of revenue**.
- Recruit **5–8 active pilot operations**, with at least **3 using BluRok weekly**.

### Team and funding

- Founder/CEO/Product Lead
- Founding/Lead Engineer
- Applied AI/Data Engineer
- Fractional design, cultivation, regulatory, and security specialists
- **Budget: $325,000**

### Horizon 1 exit gate

BluRok can demonstrate that producer responses and outcomes are being captured reliably, recommendations are evidence-grounded and governed, and real producers use the platform to support recurring operational decisions.

## Horizon 2 — Next: Cross-Domain Profitability Intelligence (3–9 months)

*Connect operational data across the farm so BluRok can determine which decisions improve biological, quality, operational, and financial outcomes.*

### Product priorities

- Connect environmental, cultivation, intervention, yield, quality, labor, cost, sales, and market data.
- Launch cross-domain profitability intelligence.
- Launch the evidence-grounded **“What should I produce next?”** workflow.
- Integrate priority sensor, accounting, compliance, POS, and marketplace systems.
- Add expert review for uncertain, regulated, or high-consequence recommendations.
- Track cultivar, strain, production-cycle, and intervention performance.
- Test hybrid B2B pricing with a monthly platform fee plus premium usage, integrations, or completed decision workflows.

### Success metrics

- At least **70%** of pilot production cycles contain complete linked outcome records.
- Verified Outcome Rate increases to **≥70%**.
- Production-planning decision time decreases by **≥30%**.
- At least **70%** of pilot users rate recommendations useful or highly useful.
- Launch at least **3 priority integrations** with **≥95% successful imports**.
- Convert at least **10 operations into paying customers**.
- Maintain **≥70% 90-day retention**.
- Reach at least **$5,000 MRR** and **$500 blended monthly ARPU**.
- Demonstrate measurable improvement from at least **2 decision modules**.
- Maintain gross margin of **≥75%**.

### Team and funding

- Founder/CEO/Product Lead
- Founding/Lead Engineer
- Applied AI/Data Engineer
- Backend/Integrations Engineer
- Pilot implementation/customer-success support
- Fractional domain specialists
- **Budget: $875,000**

### Horizon 2 exit gate

BluRok can prove that connecting operational and financial data produces better decisions, measurable customer value, repeat usage, retention, and willingness to pay commercial B2B prices.

## Horizon 3 — Bet: Network Intelligence and Workflow Orchestration (9–18 months)

*Turn accumulated farm and market outcomes into a privacy-safe intelligence network that improves as more producers use BluRok.*

### Product priorities

- Launch privacy-safe network intelligence.
- Provide comparable-producer performance benchmarks.
- Build farm-specific predictive models from longitudinal operating history.
- Connect producer outcomes with consumer preferences, purchases, repeat purchases, pricing, and sell-through.
- Create producer-to-market feedback loops.
- Expand from recommendations into human-approved workflow orchestration.
- Support multi-site and international producer operations.
- Prepare the product, economics, and data advantage for the next institutional funding round.

### Success metrics

- Network recommendations outperform farm-only and general-purpose AI baselines.
- Comparable-producer benchmarks do not expose identifiable producer information.
- Workflow orchestration reduces manual operational work by **≥25%**.
- Maintain **zero unauthorized consequential actions**.
- Reach **30–50 paying operations**.
- Reach **$20,000–$50,000 MRR**.
- Demonstrate that proprietary outcome data materially improves recommendation accuracy, personalization, or profitability.
- Establish repeatable producer onboarding, implementation, retention, and expansion processes.

### Team and funding

- Founder/CEO/Product Lead
- Three core engineers
- A fourth Product/Frontend or Platform Engineer added only after commercial validation
- Customer success/implementation support
- Fractional security, regulatory, agronomy, and mycology experts
- **Budget: $1,050,000**
- **Operating reserve across all horizons: $250,000**

### Horizon 3 exit gate

BluRok demonstrates a defensible data advantage, repeatable commercial growth, measurable customer outcomes, and evidence that each additional operation makes the product more valuable.

## Board Pitch

**Thesis (1 sentence):**
BluRok Vision helps commercial growers make better operating decisions and turns each verified decision and outcome into proprietary farm intelligence that compounds over time.

**The case:**

1. Why now: The strategy does not yet contain enough external evidence to claim a specific 6–12 month market inflection, so we should not manufacture one. The strategic window we can defend is narrower: general-purpose AI capabilities are becoming easier to access and cheaper to reproduce, which means the durable advantage is shifting away from the interface and toward proprietary operational, biological, economic, and outcome data. BluRok needs to capture that farm-specific history now, before agricultural AI recommendations become a commodity feature.

2. What's defensible: The moat is not the copilot. The moat is the structured recommendation → producer response → action → verified outcome → correction loop. Today that moat is incomplete: the data flywheel is **10/20**, correction is weak, network effects are weak, and vendor portability is only partial. The next three horizons are designed to prove whether BluRok can convert recurring producer decisions into proprietary farm intelligence that materially improves future recommendations. OpenAI or another platform can reproduce the interface in six months; it cannot instantly reproduce years of farm-specific operating history, corrections, yield and quality outcomes, labor data, economics, and producer behavior. If that data does not make BluRok measurably better over time, the moat thesis fails.

3. The economics: **81.1% modeled AI-adjusted gross margin**, with approximately **$39.74 contribution per user per month** under the current $49 ARPU assumption. AI serving cost is modeled at approximately **$3.26 per user per month**, or **$0.33 per completed production decision**, with **78% of traffic routed away from frontier models** and only **22% using frontier capacity**. At approximately **$125,000 in monthly fixed costs**, modeled break-even is roughly **3,146 paying users at $49 ARPU**. That is the uncomfortable part of the current model: the economics survive inference cost, but the proposed price point requires significant scale. We therefore need to validate whether commercial producers will support materially higher B2B pricing before treating the current break-even model as investable.

**The risks:**

1. Trust / failure modes: The front-page failure is a confident recommendation that causes crop loss, an unsafe pesticide application, regulatory exposure, or a material financial loss. BluRok's reliability contract is a **92% weekly accuracy target on the validated gold set**, visible uncertainty, tiered confidence, and mandatory human review when confidence falls below defined thresholds or when AI evidence conflicts with farm records, sensors, SOPs, or the user. The weakness is that the current gold set is only **14 cases—7 standard and 7 adversarial**. That is an evaluation starting point, not sufficient evidence of production reliability. We should treat 92% as a gate to validate, not a claim of proven performance.

2. Scale / governance: At 10x usage, the primary failure points are inference cost, review capacity, data quality, model drift, tenant isolation, latency, and unauthorized autonomy. BluRok therefore remains an advisory system: agents may observe, retrieve, summarize, calculate, rank, recommend, and draft, but consequential decisions require defined approval owners and escalation triggers. Horizon 1 must prove governance, traceability, outcome capture, and recurring producer use before Horizon 2 expands commercial complexity; Horizon 3 should not introduce broader orchestration until those controls hold under real production load.

3. Competitive: The scenario that forces a kill is not OpenAI launching agricultural recommendations; we should assume platforms can do that. The kill scenario is that, after **3–6 months of real producer use**, BluRok's proprietary farm history does not produce meaningfully better decisions than general-purpose AI, users do not repeatedly rely on it for operational decisions, measurable outcomes do not improve, or willingness to pay does not support sustainable economics. In that scenario, the data advantage is not compounding and we should pivot rather than continue funding replaceable AI features.

**The ask:**
Invest **$2.5 million over 18 months** to fund up to **4 engineers and 1 product manager**, with progress reviewed quarterly against explicit adoption, reliability, economic, and data-advantage gates. The capital funds three sequential proofs: Horizon 1 proves reliable outcome capture, governed recommendations, and recurring producer usage; Horizon 2 proves measurable customer value, retention, willingness to pay, and the value of connected operational and financial data; Horizon 3 proves that BluRok's proprietary data materially outperforms generic AI and supports repeatable commercial growth. If funded, we pause broad feature expansion, premature autonomy, and speculative network features until the preceding proof points are achieved.
